    How to settle a fine quickly

    1. Search the offence Use the dashboard search to confirm the clause, amount, and whether a matching notice is open for you.
    2. Open the fine details Select Pay now from Your open fines to review the current amount, due date, and payment options.
    3. Complete payment in your bank app Use Open Banking or bank transfer from the SCN page so the record stays tied to the correct notice.
