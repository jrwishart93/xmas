How it works

Alleged Breach Reporting

On the Cases page any team member can:

  • Choose the team member involved
  • Choose the relevant section of the Act
  • Add optional notes about what happened
  • Send the report

The accused sees it under “Needs you” on the Cases page and on their dashboard. They then have two options:

Accept it

They pay the amount for that section straight away in Monzo. If it isn’t paid within the time the Act allows, it doubles.

Contest it

The case goes to a 48-hour team vote. Everyone except the accused and the person who reported it can vote.

If more people vote guilty than not guilty, the amount is doubled.

Otherwise (including a tie, or nobody voting) the case is not upheld and the person who reported it pays £1 court costs to the team fund.

This system is designed to provide a transparent and fair way of managing social contributions within the team.
