What’s Coming

Alleged Breach Reporting

A new feature is currently in development which will allow team members to formally report an alleged breach of the Social Contributions Act.

When released, users will be able to:

  • Select the team member involved
  • Choose the relevant section of the Act
  • Add optional notes or circumstances
  • Submit the allegation for review

Once submitted, the accused team member will receive a notification and the matter will appear on the Outstanding Offences Board.

The accused will then have two options:

Admit the offence

The contribution can be paid immediately using the Quick Payment options above.

Contest the allegation

The matter will be placed before the team for a vote.

If the vote finds the accused guilty, the contribution amount will be doubled.

If the vote finds the accused not guilty, the allegation will be dismissed and the reporting member will be required to pay £1 court costs to the team fund.

This system is designed to provide a transparent and fair way of managing social contributions within the team.
